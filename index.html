How to play

1. Tap an arrow to slide it out, like a snake.

2. An arrow can leave only if nothing is in front of its head.

3. A wrong tap costs one life. You start with 3 lives.

4. Hint highlights one arrow you can remove. 3 hints per level.

5. Win a level to get 50 coins. Restart after losing costs 60 coins.

6. Harder levels are marked Tricky or Hard. Every 7th level is Hard.

7. Pinch with two fingers to zoom the board. Drag with one finger to move it.
